# Delve, Die, Repeat 2.5.0

## Headline
Achievements are here: 77 of them to earn, and with Compete on they also unlock in Google Play Games for XP.

## Interface
- Earning an achievement puts a card on the rail, and anything your death earned shows on the death screen.
- ☰ ACHIEVEMENTS lists every one with your progress, and a few stay secret until you stumble into them.
- With Compete on and signed in, VIEW IN PLAY GAMES at the top of that list opens Play's own achievements screen.
- With Compete on, your achievement progress is sent to Play Games, including anything you earned while it was off.
