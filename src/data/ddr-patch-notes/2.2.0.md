# Delve, Die, Repeat 2.2.0

## Headline
The leaderboard is now ours, not Google's. LEADERBOARD shows everyone's dead; YOUR DEAD shows just yours, ranked by DEPTH, DAYS, KILLS or WILMST with RACE and SUB-CLASS filters. Season of the Alpha begins.
- A rolled @handle stands in for you, no account needed; re-roll it any time.
- ERASE MY RUNS wipes your board history for good.
- Compete OFF sends nothing to the board, ever.

## Interface
- Leaderboards: a new panel, one board for everyone's dead (LEADERBOARD) or just yours (YOUR DEAD), a header box that switches views, tap a row for the cause of death, epitaph, six stat chips and the date and version; your best run outside the top ten pins under NOT IN THE TOP TEN · YOUR BEST; an empty filter shows NOBODY YET with CLEAR FILTERS.
- The old boards (Google's leaderboards): gone → a rolled @handle (with its own initials) stands in for you, no account needed; RE-ROLL HANDLE and ERASE MY RUNS live in the ☰ account block and the title's corner chip, next to COMPETE ON/OFF.
- A Compete-ON death now shows "You placed Nth of M." on the death screen, once the board answers.
- Runs played with Compete off: never reach the board, even after Compete goes back on.
- REPORT A BUG: unlimited → a short cooldown and a daily cap; a refusal keeps your draft so you can send it later.
- The ☰ menu: the CENTRE MAP row is gone; MAKE CAMP moves to the top.
- YOUR DEAD starts from runs played on 2.1.0 onward; older graves are not shown.
